# Morganics Shopify Content Guide

This file documents the content system for the Morganics Shopify website: product copy, SEO, metafields, Urdu/English naming, homepage section copy, collection copy, safety rules, tone, and reusable writing templates.

## Purpose

Use this file when writing, editing, importing, or reviewing Morganics website content.

Content should help customers understand:

- What the product is.
- How it is used.
- Why it belongs in a pantry or wellness routine.
- How to store it.
- What precautions matter.
- Whether it is a food, seed, spice, herb, resin, dry fruit, or specialty item.

The content should support ecommerce conversion without making unsafe health claims.

## Brand Voice

Morganics content should sound:

- Practical
- Natural
- Trustworthy
- Warm
- Clear
- Pakistani-market friendly
- Useful for everyday customers

Avoid:

- Overpromising.
- Medical claims.
- Complicated scientific language.
- Generic AI-sounding filler.
- Repeating the same sentence across every product.
- Exaggerated miracle-benefit wording.

## Language Style

Morganics uses mixed English and Urdu product naming.

Preferred title format:

```text
English Product Name – اردو نام
```

Examples:

```text
Aqarqara – عقرقرحا
Kajo Roasted – کاجو روسٹڈ
Maghaz Sunflower Seeds – مغز سورج مکھی
```

If a product has a common English equivalent, use:

```text
English Name — Common English Name – اردو نام
```

Example:

```text
Anjeer — Fig – انجیر
```

Rules:

- Keep titles readable.
- Do not overload titles with claims.
- Keep Urdu spelling consistent where possible.
- Do not add unnecessary promotional words to titles.

## Product Description Structure

Recommended product description format:

```text
Product Name

Short intro paragraph

Why use this product?

Key benefits

How to consume / use

Storage

Precautions

FAQs
```

Keep descriptions useful and specific.

## Product Description Template

Use this template when writing a product description:

```text
[Product Name] by Morganics is selected for customers who want a clean, practical natural ingredient for everyday pantry use, traditional routines, and balanced lifestyle support.

Why use this product?
[Product Name] can be used as part of regular food, pantry, herbal, spice, or wellness routines depending on the product type. It is selected for freshness, natural character, and practical daily use.

Key benefits
- Clean single-ingredient product selected for practical use.
- Useful in Pakistani kitchens, pantry routines, and traditional preparations.
- Freshness-focused packing helps preserve natural aroma, taste, and quality.
- Easy to use in sensible portions.
- Suitable for customers who prefer simple, natural ingredients.

How to consume / use
[Write specific usage based on product type.]

Storage
Keep sealed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight and moisture. After opening, store in an airtight container. Refrigerate nuts, seeds, and dried fruits in hot weather.

Precautions
Use in sensible quantities. If pregnant, nursing, allergic, taking medication, or managing a medical condition, consult a qualified professional before using concentrated herbs or specialty products.

FAQs
1. Q: What is [Product Name] used for?
   A: It is used as a food, herb, spice, seed, dry fruit, or wellness ingredient depending on its category.

2. Q: How do I use it?
   A: Follow the usage guidance above and use sensible portions.

3. Q: Can it be used daily?
   A: Food items can usually be used in sensible portions. Concentrated herbs and specialty products should be used carefully.

4. Q: How should I store it?
   A: Keep sealed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight and moisture.

5. Q: Is this a medicine?
   A: No. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
```

## Product Type Usage Guidance

### Nuts And Dry Fruits

Use wording around:

- Freshness.
- Taste.
- Texture.
- Snacking.
- Pantry use.
- Baking.
- Desserts.
- Breakfast.
- Gifting.

Safe benefit examples:

```text
Good for everyday snacking and pantry use.
Adds natural texture and richness to recipes.
Useful for breakfast bowls, desserts, and traditional dishes.
```

Avoid:

```text
Cures weakness.
Boosts fertility.
Treats heart disease.
Guaranteed energy.
```

### Seeds

Use wording around:

- Salads.
- Smoothies.
- Cereals.
- Baking.
- Roasting.
- Fiber.
- Texture.
- Sensible portions.

Safe benefit examples:

```text
Adds texture and natural plant-based nutrition to daily meals.
Can be used in smoothies, salads, roti dough, cereals, and baking.
Best used in small daily portions with enough water.
```

### Herbs

Use wording around:

- Traditional use.
- Herbal routines.
- Tea/decoction if appropriate.
- Aroma.
- Careful use.
- Practitioner advice for concentrated products.

Safe benefit examples:

```text
Traditionally used in herbal routines.
Suitable for customers who prefer natural ingredients for careful, practical use.
Use in small quantities according to traditional practice or practitioner guidance.
```

### Spices

Use wording around:

- Aroma.
- Flavor.
- Cooking.
- Chai.
- Desserts.
- Traditional recipes.

Safe benefit examples:

```text
Adds warm aroma and flavor to traditional recipes.
Useful in chai, desserts, spice blends, and home cooking.
Freshness-focused packing helps preserve aroma.
```

### Resins And Specialty Items

Examples:

```text
Luban
Goond Katira
Salajeet
Musabbar
Harmal
```

Use more cautious wording:

- Traditional use.
- Specialty ingredient.
- Use carefully.
- Avoid daily casual use unless appropriate.
- Consult qualified professional where needed.

Avoid strong health claims.

### Short Description

Purpose:

- Quick summary near product page/card if used.

Template:

```text
Clean natural [product type] selected for freshness, practical use, and everyday pantry or traditional routines.
```

### Benefits

Purpose:

- Short, safe, customer-friendly benefit statement.

Example:

```text
Supports everyday pantry use, natural recipe preparation, and balanced lifestyle routines.
```

Avoid disease claims.

### Use Cases

Purpose:

- Where/how the product fits into customer life.

Example:

```text
Daily pantry use, traditional recipes, smoothies, breakfast bowls, baking, herbal routines.
```

### Usage

Purpose:

- Practical use instructions.

Examples:

```text
Use 1-2 tablespoons daily in smoothies, salads, roti dough, cereals, or baking.
```

```text
Use a small quantity in chai, desserts, spice blends, or traditional recipes.
```

```text
Use carefully in small quantities according to traditional practice or practitioner guidance.
```

### Precautions

Purpose:

- Safety and responsible-use note.

Template:

```text
Use in sensible quantities. If pregnant, nursing, allergic, taking medication, or managing a medical condition, consult a qualified professional before use. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
```

For seeds:

```text
Seed allergy caution. Start with small portions and drink enough water with high-fiber seeds. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
```

For nuts:

```text
Nut allergy caution. Store properly after opening. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
```

## Safety And Compliance Rules

Do not claim any product:

- Cures disease.
- Treats disease.
- Prevents disease.
- Diagnoses disease.
- Guarantees a health result.
- Replaces medicine.

Avoid these risky words unless used in the required disclaimer:

```text
cure
treat
treatment
cancer
diabetes
testosterone
fertility
sexual
disease
detox
weight loss
blood pressure
cholesterol
guaranteed
miracle
```

Required safe disclaimer where needed:

```text
This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
```

Preferred safe wording:

```text
traditional use
pantry use
everyday recipes
balanced diet
sensible portions
natural ingredient
freshness
aroma
texture
wellness routine
practical use
```

## Repetition Control

Avoid making every product description identical.

Vary content by product category:

- Nuts: texture, snacking, desserts, breakfast.
- Seeds: smoothies, salads, fiber, baking, roasting.
- Herbs: traditional routines, careful use, teas if appropriate.
- Spices: aroma, chai, recipes, desserts.
- Dates: natural sweetness, snacking, gifting.
- Resins/specialty: cautious traditional use.

## Content QA Checklist

Before publishing product content:

1. Product title is clear.
2. Urdu/English title format is consistent.
3. Description explains actual use.
4. Usage instructions are practical.
5. Storage instruction is included.
6. Precaution is included.
7. No unsafe medical claims.
8. SEO title is readable.
9. SEO description is safe.
10. Product metafields are filled where needed.
11. FAQs do not overpromise.
12. Product category language matches the actual item.

## Bulk Content Update Workflow

For many products:

1. Export products from Shopify Admin.
2. Edit content in CSV carefully.
3. Keep product handles unchanged.
4. Fill descriptions and SEO fields.
5. Import a small test batch first.
6. Review product pages.
7. Import the rest.
8. Check live products and collection cards.

For metafields, use Shopify Admin bulk editor when fields are available as columns.

## Final Content Standard

Every product should answer:

```text
What is it?
Why would a customer buy it?
How do they use it?
How should they store it?
What should they be careful about?
Is the content safe and non-medical?
```

If content does not answer these questions, improve it before publishing.
